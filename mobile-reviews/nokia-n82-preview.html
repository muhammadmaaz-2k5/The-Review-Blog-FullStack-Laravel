Introduction

Launched in 2007, the Nokia N82 was a multimedia smartphone featuring a 5-megapixel camera with Carl Zeiss optics, xenon flash, GPS navigation, and comprehensive multimedia features. It was designed as a camera-focused smartphone that combined excellent photography capabilities with full smartphone functionality.

It was one of the best camera smartphones of 2007.

Availability & Historical Pricing

Global release in Q4 2007. Unsubsidized price was around €450–550 — positioned as a premium camera smartphone.

Design & Build Quality

  • Dimensions: 112 × 50.2 × 17.3 mm
  • Weight: 114 g
  • Colours: Black, Silver

The N82 featured a candybar design with dedicated camera controls and lens cover. Build quality was excellent, and the design was optimized for photography while maintaining smartphone functionality.

Display

Display Specs
Size 2.4 inches TFT, 240 × 320 pixels, 16M colors
Type QVGA resolution

The display was sharp and vibrant, perfect for viewing photos and navigating the Symbian interface.

Camera Performance

  • Main: 5 MP, Carl Zeiss optics, autofocus, xenon flash
  • Video: VGA @ 30fps

The 5-megapixel camera with Carl Zeiss optics and xenon flash was exceptional for 2007, producing excellent photos in both daylight and low-light conditions. The combination of Carl Zeiss optics and xenon flash made it one of the best camera phones available.

Multimedia & Features

GPS navigation with A-GPS, Wi-Fi, MP3 player, FM radio, microSD slot (up to 8GB), video playback, comprehensive multimedia codec support, and extensive Symbian applications.

Performance & Software

Ran Symbian S60 3rd Edition Feature Pack 1 — fast and reliable. The phone handled camera operations, GPS navigation, and multimedia playback effortlessly.

Battery Life & Connectivity

  • Battery: 1050 mAh Li-Ion
  • Talk time: Up to 4 hours
  • Standby: Up to 280 hours
  • Connectivity: 3G (HSDPA), Wi-Fi, GPS, Bluetooth 2.0, USB 2.0

Battery life was adequate — lasting 1–2 days with moderate use. GPS and camera usage could drain the battery faster.

Verdict & Legacy

The Nokia N82 was an excellent camera smartphone that combined the best of both worlds: exceptional camera capabilities with Carl Zeiss optics and xenon flash, plus comprehensive smartphone features including GPS navigation and Wi-Fi. It was one of the most complete camera smartphones of 2007, offering excellent photography without sacrificing smartphone functionality.

For users in 2007, the N82 offered a compelling package for photography enthusiasts who also wanted smartphone capabilities. The combination of Carl Zeiss optics and xenon flash made it stand out in the crowded camera phone market, while GPS navigation and Wi-Fi ensured it remained competitive as a smartphone.

The device's legacy is that of a camera smartphone that didn't compromise. It demonstrated that excellent camera capabilities could be combined with comprehensive smartphone features, setting a standard for future camera phones.

A camera smartphone that delivered photographic excellence without compromise.
